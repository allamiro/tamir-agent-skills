# FBA checklist before trusting a result

1. Every reaction is element- and charge-balanced, or deliberately unbalanced (exchange, biomass, demand).
2. The medium is set: uptake bounds for every nutrient, all other uptakes closed.
3. With every uptake closed, the objective is zero (no free energy or mass).
4. The ATP maintenance requirement is set if the organism has one.
5. The objective is justified for the organism and condition, with a reference.
6. FVA has been run at the optimum (or at a stated fraction of it).
7. No loop carries flux at the optimum, or loopless FBA was used.
8. Predictions were compared with at least one measured rate not used to set the bounds.
9. The model version, solver and tolerance are recorded.

Source: general constraint-based modelling practice, author knowledge, VERIFY against MEMOTE's test list
and the COBRA documentation.
