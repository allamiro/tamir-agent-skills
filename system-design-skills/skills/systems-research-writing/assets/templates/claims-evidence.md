## Checks

- [ ] Each contribution has evidence, and each experiment supports a contribution.
- [ ] Every number in the abstract and introduction appears in the evaluation with its setup.
- [ ] Baselines are tuned fairly and the settings are listed.
- [ ] Runs, variance and the noise floor are reported.
- [ ] Limitations name what was not tested.
- [ ] References checked; artifact described; contributions and tool use recorded.
