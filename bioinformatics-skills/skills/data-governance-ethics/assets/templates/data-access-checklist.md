## Handling

- [ ] Storage location is approved and access-controlled.
- [ ] Only named, approved people have access, and the list is dated.
- [ ] Identifiers are absent from file names, code, logs, history and notes.
- [ ] No data are sent to an online or automated tool without written permission.
- [ ] Retention and deletion date recorded.
- [ ] Small cell counts checked before anything is published.
