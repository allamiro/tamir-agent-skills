# Confidence scale

Grade each word on the evidence for it, not on how natural the translation sounds.

| Grade | Meaning | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| A | Reading and word both secure | Each sign value from a sign list, the word in a dictionary entry the user trusts, spelling matches |
| B | Reading secure, word chosen from context | Signs certain; the word is a good fit but not the only candidate |
| C | Reading or segmentation uncertain | At least one sign has two values that give different words, or a boundary is a guess |
| D | Damaged or restored | A sign is missing or partly preserved; the reading rests on a restoration |

Line grade = weakest word grade. Text grade = list of lines at each grade, never an average.

Escalate to a specialist when any word is C or D and the translation depends on it.
