# Gardiner sections

The list sorts signs into 26 sections by what they depict. The sections overlap: Gardiner says
the classes are not mutually exclusive (S, T, U and V especially), so a sign's section tells you
its picture, not its job. Section names below are short headings of the list.

| Letter | Section | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| A | Man and his occupations | people, actions, many determinatives |
| B | Woman and her occupations | female persons |
| C | Anthropomorphic deities | gods |
| D | Parts of the human body | eye, mouth, arm, leg; many phonetic signs |
| E | Mammals | animals |
| F | Parts of mammals | heads, skins, organs |
| G | Birds | vulture, owl, quail chick; several uniliterals |
| H | Parts of birds | wing, feather, egg |
| I | Amphibious animals, reptiles, etc. | viper, cobra, crocodile |
| K | Fishes and parts of fishes | |

| M | Trees and plants | reed, papyrus, lotus |
| N | Sky, earth, water | sun, water, land |
| O | Buildings, parts of buildings, etc. | house, enclosure |
| P | Ships and parts of ships | |
