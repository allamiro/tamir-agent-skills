## Searching tips

- Search names under conventional English, German and French spellings and, where relevant, under the
  transliteration.
- Search a monument by its site and by its museum or inventory number.
- Chase footnotes and bibliographies in both directions: back to what the author cites, forward to later
  reviews and responses.
- Reviews of an edition often record corrections that the edition does not.

## Reading an Egyptological reference

A typical reference gives author, title, series or journal with volume, place and year, then pages or
plates. Journal and series titles are heavily abbreviated; resolve each abbreviation from the abbreviation
list in the same publication or a reference work before relying on it. Do not expand an abbreviation from
memory.
