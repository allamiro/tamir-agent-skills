# Sign roles

| Role | Meaning | Example (from the curated table) |
|---|---|---|
| Uniliteral | One consonant | D21 mouth = r |
| Biliteral | Two consonants | D2 face = ḥr |
| Triliteral | Three consonants | F35 heart with windpipe = nfr |
| Ideogram | Writes the word for the thing shown | N5 sun = rꜥ "sun, day" |
| Phonetic complement | A uniliteral that repeats one consonant of a multiliteral beside it | a second consonant after a biliteral |
| Determinative | Gives the word's category; has no sound | A1 seated man after a word for a man's role |
| Marker | Stroke or plural strokes | Z1 marks an ideogram; Z2 marks plurality |

Rules of thumb (each can fail):

- A determinative comes last in its word. A word can have more than one.
- Sound signs read in the order written, except where a sign is placed first for honorific
  reasons. This is not in the supplied list: VERIFY with a grammar.
- Gardiner notes that these labels are used loosely; do not treat the boundaries as sharp.
